No complicated onboarding process. Just a clear conversation about what you need and how we can support you.

02

Built Around You

Your operation comes first.

We don't believe every landlord, investor or estate agent needs the same type of support. That's why our services are structured around your actual workload.

  • Start with what you need. Focus on the tasks creating the most pressure.
  • Keep communication simple. Use agreed communication channels and processes.
  • Track the work. Keep tasks, requests and follow-ups organised.
  • Scale when necessary. Increase your support as your portfolio or workload grows.

What happens before we start?

Before providing ongoing support, we'll establish a clear scope so both sides understand what is being handled.

  • Services required
  • Number of properties or expected workload
  • Communication process
  • Responsibilities
  • Reporting requirements
  • Monthly support package
