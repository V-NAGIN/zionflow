How it works

Ordering takes three simple steps.

1

Choose your product

Select the item you want and click the WhatsApp button. Your message will already include the product name.

2

Confirm the details

We confirm availability, product specifications, delivery details and the final amount with you before payment.

3

Complete your order

Once everything is confirmed, we provide the available payment method and proceed with your order.

Before you order

Everything should be clear before you pay.

Delivery

Delivery time depends on the product, supplier and destination. We confirm the expected delivery timeframe before payment. If a specific product has a longer lead time, we will tell you before you place the order.

Payment

Do not send payment until your order details have been confirmed with Zionflow Shop. The available payment method and final amount will be provided during the WhatsApp ordering process.

Product specifications

Product specifications can vary by supplier or model. We confirm the relevant specifications and availability before payment so you know what you are ordering.

Returns & problems

If your order arrives damaged, incorrect or has another issue, contact us on WhatsApp as soon as possible. We will review the situation and explain the applicable replacement or refund process.

Important

Product availability, specifications, delivery estimates and delivery charges can change. The details confirmed with you before payment are the details that apply to your order.

FAQ

Questions before ordering?

How do I place an order?

Click any "Buy via WhatsApp" button. WhatsApp will open with a pre-written message containing the product you selected. We'll then confirm the order details with you.

Do I pay immediately?

No. First confirm the product, availability, delivery information and final amount with us. Only then should you proceed with the payment method we provide.

How much is delivery?

Delivery charges depend on the product and destination. The applicable delivery cost will be confirmed before payment.

Can I buy more than one product?

Yes. Send us a WhatsApp message listing the products you want and we'll confirm availability and the combined order total.

What if I have a question about a product?

Message us before ordering. We can confirm the available product information with you before you make a payment.

Can I track my order?

Where tracking is available for the selected delivery method, tracking information will be provided after the order has been dispatched.
